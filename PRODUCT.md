# TicketChain
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User-specified React, TypeScript, Vite and ethers v6.
## Users
Students demonstrating a ticket service; buyers, event administrator and assigned entry checkers.
## Product Purpose
Find events, purchase a numbered seat, show a ticket and record entry on Ethereum. Demonstrate cancellations and refunds with test ETH.
## Operating Context
Kazakh/Russian web application, MetaMask and Sepolia. Local EVM verification precedes wallet-signed testnet deployment.
## Capabilities and Constraints
The approved attached specification defines six routes, nontransferable tickets, per-event escrow, owner refunds, QR identification and organizer/checker permissions. The contract is the authority for all money and inventory.
## Brand Commitments
Own TicketChain identity; light background, dark text, one bright accent. Ticketon is a user-flow reference only.
## Evidence on Hand
Existing Solidity source and user requirements. No genuine public events or deployed Sepolia address supplied. Demo metadata must be labeled as examples.
## Accessibility and Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, labeled fields, visible focus, 360 px mobile layout, Russian/Kazakh translations.
