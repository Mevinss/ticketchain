# TicketChain interface

User-approved direction: a light ticket catalog with large poster art, dark readable text and one warm accent. The layout supports buying and entry operations, not a marketing-only page.

Palette: paper #f6f5f0, white #ffffff, ink #242721, secondary #666960, divider #dedfd5, accent #bd4124, sage hero #e9eddf.
Typeface: self-hosted Noto Sans (full Kazakh Cyrillic support), 400/500/600/700/800. Display 38–59 px, body 13–16 px; minimum interactive control height 44 px where space permits.
Container: 1248 px, 36 px desktop gutters, 18 px mobile. Catalog: three/two/one columns. 14 px cards, 8 px buttons. No background decoration behind forms.
Signature: editorial poster typography and a physical ticket-shaped hero. Real on-chain event titles, dates and prices always replace examples after configuration. Seed candidates were constrained by the user's specified layout; monochrome strobing/data and skewed timetable challengers were rejected for accessibility and task clarity.
State: transaction progress is a separate persistent region; no purchase success before a receipt. Seat selected/sold states use labels and glyphs in addition to color. Keyboard focus: 3 px accent outline. Respect reduced motion.
Screens: catalog, detail/seat map, wallet tickets, printable QR ticket, organizer forms, entry checker. Forms preserve semantic labels and error messages.
